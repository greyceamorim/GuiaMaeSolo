# Guia da Mãe Solo

**A free guide that explains, in plain language, the rights and benefits available to single parents in Brazil, and how to ask for them.**

Versão em português: [README.pt-BR.md](README.pt-BR.md)

## What it does
- Asks 7 simple questions: children's ages, what you need, state, household size and income.
- Shows only what applies to you: daycare and school place rights, care outside school hours, income-based benefits and child support, with where to go and what to bring.
- Calculates household income per person and shows whether you are within each benefit's limit.
- Lets you tick off steps and send the list by WhatsApp.

## What it does not do
It does not find or reserve places, give legal advice or guarantee any benefit. Rules change: always confirm with CRAS, the city hall or the Defensoria Pública.

## Privacy
No ads, no cookies, no sign-up. Your answers, including income, stay on your phone. Only the optional feedback ("Este guia ajudou?") is stored: yes or no, the step codes shown, and an optional comment.

## Why it exists
Rights and benefits exist, but the information is scattered and hard to understand, and the bureaucracy is heavy. This project exists to make that information available to everyone, in simple language, and to show the way to ask for it.

It starts with single mothers because, according to the 2022 PNAD Contínua (IBGE), analysed by Ipea:

- About **10.9 million single mothers** are the household head, **86.5%** of single-parent families.
- Their average labour income is **R$ 2,322**, the lowest of the groups compared.
- Daycare attendance for ages 0 to 3 was **39.7% in 2024**, below the 50% target (IBGE).

These are national figures: they show many families rely on one income, not the cause of their difficulties. Sources: [Ipea, Boletim Mercado de Trabalho nº 81](https://repositorio.ipea.gov.br/server/api/core/bitstreams/135daea9-b788-4eb2-afb4-9a51a30581c7/content) and [IBGE, early childhood education 2024](https://agenciadenoticias.ibge.gov.br/agencia-noticias/2012-agencia-de-noticias/noticias/45338-indicadores-da-educacao-infantil-avancam-mas-nao-atingem-meta-do-pne). "Mãe Solo" is the term families use; the guide also works for single fathers.
